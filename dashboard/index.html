Kronos Foundation-Model Forecasts
Probabilistic candle-path forecasts (open-weight Kronos, arxiv:2508.02739). Each card = one instrument/timeframe. Direction gauge = share of sampled paths closing up. Distribution strip = terminal-price spread (p10→p90) with today's close ● and TP/SL levels. Barrier bar = P(TP-first) vs P(SL-first). NOT a standalone trade signal — a regime/confluence input.

Shadow Gate — realised evidence
Every paper entry is annotated with the Kronos forecast it agreed/disagreed with (log-only, never gates). This panel shows how annotated trades actually closed, and what P&L the simulated gate (skip entries a fresh forecast disagrees with at ≥60% confidence) WOULD have produced. Real gating is only considered when the verdict is GATE IMPROVES and calibration is trusted.

Polymarket Paper-EV

Only markets resolving within the Kronos forecast horizon are priced. [TAIL] = market_prob<5% (inflated EV is model tail-noise, not edge). Same-date band bets are correlated.
